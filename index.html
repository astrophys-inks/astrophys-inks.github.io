FAQs/Info

Answers to frequently asked questions! If your question is not answered you can contact me here.

How do I book?

Visit the booking page and fill out a form! If my books are currently closed it will say so there!

If books are closed, how can I join a waitlist?

See the waitlists page for updates.

Can I use your design and get tattooed by someone else?

Yes but only if you buy a tattoo ticket for $25, can be paid via Venmo, Apple Pay, or Zelle! You can email me to get an official tattoo ticket. If you use my design without paying me I will be so sad and your tattoo will be so cursed (and probably ugly!)

Can you draw me a design to get tattooed by someone else?

Yes! You can email me to commission a custom design. It is $50 and can be paid via Venmo, Apple Pay, or Zelle.

Can you tattoo a design drawn by someone else?

No, I will not tattoo other artists' designs/flash even with their permission. I am happy to draw you something inspired by someone else's work but is our own new design!

I'm in town for the next couple days! Can I get a tattoo from you? Do you take walk-ins?

Most likely no :/ my books fill up about a month in advance! If you're planning travel to Oahu and want to book with me, please plan in advance! Feel free to email me about special requests.

When do you work?

Every day! But my office job M-F usually keeps me out of the studio. I typically only book tattoos on weekends, with some exceptions for projects I really want to pursue!

How much do you charge?

My minimum for flash is $200 and for custom work is $250. I do not have a set hourly rate; I charge by the piece. Factors that are considered in pricing are design, placement (and to some extent the condition of your skin), and size.

What payment methods do you accept?

I can accept Venmo, Apple Pay, or Zelle for digital options. I much prefer cash for the remainder due at your appointment, though!

Can I get my deposit back?

No :( <3 (I do make some exceptions sometimes tho)

Booking Info + Form

BOOKS ARE CURRENTLY CLOSED. NOVEMBER BOOKS OPEN OCTOBER 5th, 10AM.

My booking procedure goes as follows: The booking form is published the first week of the month for the following month (for example, Oct 5th for November appointments). I leave the form open for about a day or two or until I have enough responses. Flash requests are always filled first, in the order they are received. Then I go through custom requests and decide which ones are best suited for me, in the time I have left. If your request is not able to be fulfilled, I will email you to let you know! The same general procedure is used for guest spots, and each guest spot will have a separate booking form link on this page.

The appointment deposit is $100 and can be sent via Venmo, Apple Pay, or Zelle. I will send specific instructions once we discuss your appointment. The deposit is NON-REFUNDABLE so please be sure you’re ready to commit to your appointment time.

For specific flash days, just email me to inquire about an appointment! No need to fill out the form.
