# Astral for astrophys-inks.github.io

Includes Home, Work, Contact, FAQs, Booking Form, Waitlists, and Merch. These use Astral's animated panels, with shareable addresses such as /#booking, rather than separate HTML files.

Instagram, the booking form, and both Worldwide and Local waitlists are connected to the supplied URLs. Links also work without JavaScript. Optional link settings are in assets/js/site-config.js.

Merch uses the same 12-image responsive grid as Work. Replace its sample images in index.html with product photos; wrap each image in an anchor when you have product links.

Home and Work retain the original demo text and photos. Replace your name, bio, photos, FAQ copy, and copyright in index.html. Keep the HTML5 UP design credit and LICENSE.txt.

The Contact form is connected to https://formspree.io/f/xqpalgwp and enabled. Name, email, and message are required. Formspree controls the recipient email in its dashboard. After publishing, send a test message and confirm receipt in Gmail; delivery has not been tested. The HTML form also works without JavaScript.

Copy this folder's contents into the root of astrophys-inks.github.io, keeping index.html at the root. Publish that branch and root folder through GitHub Pages. No build step is required.
